# Cost and returns

- Rent: take monthly rents from the market summary's nearby apartment buildings. Match by unit size: a studio near
  the smaller units' minimum rent, a 2BR near the larger units' maximum. Name the buildings or figures used.
- Conversion cost per square foot (hard and soft cost, rule of thumb, unconfirmed): $300 to $500 per sf for
  office-to-residential in Los Angeles. Use the middle unless the building's age or class argues otherwise.
- Operating costs: 35% of gross rent by default (rule of thumb).
- Simple yield = net operating income / conversion cost. Excludes the purchase price of the building.
- Every number is an assumption: list each one with its source.
