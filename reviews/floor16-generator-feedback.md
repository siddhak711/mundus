## 1. Hard rules (break one and the layout loses 5 points; fix these before anything else)

1. **No column may sit in an entry, a bathroom, a hallway or a door swing.** This covers detected columns *and* the hypothesised hidden ones listed in section 4. A column may sit in:
   - a demising wall or the corridor wall;
   - a kitchen or living room at least 3 m wide;
   - a closet, a wet-wall chase or the amenity zone.
2. Entries must stay at least 1.07 m (42 in) clear for their full length. Unit entry doors must be at least 0.91 m (36 in) clear.
3. Every bedroom and the main living room must be on the glass. A den or extra room counts only if it borrows light through an opening at least half the shared wall or 25 sf (CBC 1204.2.1).
4. Room sizes: one room at least 120 sf, other habitable rooms at least 70 sf, at least 7 ft in every direction. Ceilings at least 7 ft 6 in (the existing ceiling is 8 ft 9 in).
5. At least 5 units, so the project qualifies under LA's citywide adaptive reuse ordinance.

## 3. What to change from last run (in order of points gained)

1. **Make units deeper where the core is far away** (worth about +3 on rented share and +2–4 on yield).
   - The rectangular corridor loop forced every unit to about 6.3 m deep and left 5,814 sf dark.
   - Instead, run the corridor along the core and let unit depth vary with the distance from glass to core. **Cap units at 9.0 m deep.**
   - Glass-to-core distance by stretch:
     - North side: 13.2 m for x −25 to +6.6; 8.9 m for x +8 to +17.
     - South side: 11.8 m for x −25 to −19; 8.9 m for x −17 to −13; 13.3 m for x −11 to −5; 10.4 m for x −3.4 to −1.4; about 13.2 m for x +2 to +17.
     - West end: 12.6 m. East end: 11.8 m.
   - Where glass to core is about 8.9 m, keep units about 6.3 m deep plus the corridor.
   - Use the extra depth for the bathroom, laundry and a closet or den (with borrowed light).
   - Expected result: about 16,100 sf of units (76% efficiency), up from 12,453.
2. **Start from Scheme B's unit widths, about 7.5–8.5 m**, so columns land in wide rooms. Don't use A's 4.5–5.5 m micro bays.
3. **Make the four corners two-bed, two-bath**, with a bedroom on each façade.
4. **Wet rooms next to the core side and grouped in pairs.** Keep every drain run ≤8 m. The previous corners were up to 14.6 m.
5. **Draw elevator lobbies, both stairs (one per core, ≥25.7 m apart) and an exhaust chase per unit.** Measure egress to the stair doors.
6. **Keep N8-type entries away from the 4.2 m column rows.** Where a row crosses a unit, put the kitchen or a closet there, never the entry or the bathroom.
7. Any dark area left over goes to rentable storage lockers or co-working, and is labelled as earning income.
